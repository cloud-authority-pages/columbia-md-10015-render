Signs of Bed Bug Infestation You Shouldn't Ignore

Columbia residents often overlook early warning signs until infestations become severe. Small, dark spots on sheets or mattresses may appear as simple dirt, yet they represent bed bug excrement containing digested blood. Shed skins resembling translucent shells often collect along mattress seams or behind headboards. Residents may notice itchy welts appearing in linear patterns on exposed skin after sleeping, though reactions vary widely between individuals. A sweet, musty odor sometimes becomes noticeable in heavily infested rooms when large numbers of bugs release alarm pheromones. Inspecting the underside of box springs, inside nightstand drawers, and along electrical cords reveals live bugs or eggs that confirm activity. Because bed bugs can survive months without feeding, waiting to see if the problem resolves itself allows populations to expand into adjacent rooms or neighboring units. Prompt professional evaluation prevents the need for more extensive and costly interventions later. The CDC recommends contacting qualified pest management professionals at the first confirmed sign rather than attempting self-treatment that may disperse bugs further.
