Heat treatment raises ambient temperatures to levels that kill bed bugs and eggs throughout the treated space, penetrating deep into mattresses, furniture, and wall voids where chemical sprays might not reach. Chemical solutions involve EPA-approved products applied strategically to harborages and travel paths. These treatments can be more targeted and often require less preparation, but they may leave residues that some residents prefer to avoid.
